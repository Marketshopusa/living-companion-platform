## 1. Allowed use

Use `[PRODUCT_NAME]` for lawful companionship, entertainment, accessibility, language practice, and other uses that do not violate these rules or applicable law.

## 2. Prohibited — illegal and harmful activity

You may not use the service to:

- commit, plan, or solicit crimes;
- traffic illegal drugs, weapons, or stolen data;
- engage in fraud, identity theft, payment fraud, phishing, or social-engineering scams;
- launder money or evade sanctions;
- threaten violence, stalk, harass, or dox others;
- distribute malware, ransomware, or unauthorized access tools;
- sexually exploit minors, or create/seek CSAM or minor-like sexual content (zero tolerance);
- non-consensual intimate imagery (“revenge porn”);
- unauthorized cloning of a real person’s face or voice;
- violate export controls or attempt to bypass age/safety controls.

## 3. Prohibited — platform and AI abuse

You may not:

- attempt to jailbreak models to bypass safety for the purposes above;
- use Companions to impersonate `[OPERATOR_LEGAL_NAME]`, banks, governments, or real individuals to deceive others;
- scrape, bulk-extract, or resell private user data;
- attack tenant isolation, APIs, or rate limits;
- create fake accounts to evade bans;
- spam, manipulate entitlements, or reverse-engineer for competitive theft beyond lawful interoperability.

## 4. Commercial and political use

Unsolicited advertising, astroturfing, and undisclosed political campaigning via Companion channels are prohibited unless we publish a separate written permission.
